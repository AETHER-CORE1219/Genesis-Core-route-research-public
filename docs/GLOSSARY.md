# Glossary

## Research units

**Action anchor**

A passage or fixed record showing that a person performed a defined action. An action anchor is narrower than a general reputation or occupational title.

**Development420**

The fixed 420-person development analysis used to construct and examine the five non-exclusive social functions. It is not the total size of the broader route-research corpus and is not an independent replication sample.

**Exact-like**

A repository-level group for records treated as having exact or equivalent birth-time precision. Individual source labels and provenance remain important; exact-like is not a claim that every source has identical documentary quality.

**Person-out test**

A test conducted on people outside the relevant development outcome construction. In this repository, person-out does not always mean a completely untouched cohort; each release states which source, chart, or earlier prediction surfaces had prior exposure.

**Public subject trace**

A named legacy record connecting a person, field, birth-data provenance, selected chart facts, and route observations. The 761 public traces are not a public identity map for the anonymous v7 development420 matrix.

## Outcome terms

**Non-exclusive function**

An outcome that can coexist with other outcomes for the same person. A person may support advocacy, authorship, organization, technology, and competition in any combination.

**Observed function**

A social function supported by the fixed source material for a person. Failure to observe a function in that material is not proof that it never occurred in the person's life.

**Measurement regime**

The method or source-evidence regime used to determine real-world action support. Version 7 includes this as a measured adjustment factor.

## Astrology-side terms

**Exposure**

Whether a person meets a fixed chart-structure definition for a tested surface.

**Western / Jyotish ablation**

A descriptive comparison of the full both-system exposure with Western-only and Jyotish-only versions. It is not a formal interaction test.

**`pp_both_system`**

The primary-plus-primary both-system exposure used for the first four headline functions in v7.

**`any_tier_both_system`**

A broader both-system exposure. It was fixed for embodied competition because the stricter primary-plus-primary intersection was structurally zero.

## Route hierarchy

**Function group**

The broad real-world activity being measured.

**Meaning family / mechanism family**

A group of candidate branches sharing a functional role, actor capability, and meaning-coherent astrological mechanism.

**Finite route branch**

A bounded implementation path within a meaning family, retaining its relation direction, carrier, and modifiers.

**Person interpretation**

A person-specific synthesis of the full chart and several documented activities. It must not be used after the result to redefine a group-level test.

**Fixed**

Frozen for a stated public claim surface. Fixed does not mean universally true or independently replicated.

**Provisional / hold / watch**

Retained for future investigation but not part of the fixed public claim surface.

**Pressure observation**

A route signal produced by blind ingress. It is not a final identity, profession, or life label for the person.

## Statistical terms

**Risk difference**

The difference in the observed outcome rate between exposed and unexposed groups. A value of `0.336` is 33.6 percentage points.

**HC3 standard error**

A heteroskedasticity-consistent uncertainty estimate used with the linear probability model.

**Surface**

One predefined combination of social function and astrology-side exposure definition. Version 7 fixes 22 surfaces, of which 18 are estimable.

**Person-package maxT**

A simultaneous permutation procedure. Each random sign is shared across all functions for the same person, preserving within-person dependence while the maximum test statistic controls the fixed family.

**Adversarial decoy**

A weak or reverse-direction predicate reconstructed as a comparison. These decoys were selected using outcome information and are therefore not a random null distribution. They are used only as a necessary specificity challenge.

**Joint null**

The declared simultaneous chance model for the fixed family of test surfaces. Rejecting it does not remove unmeasured confounding or prove causality.

**Development-sized effect**

An effect magnitude fixed from development and used as a reference in a later test. Failure to reproduce that magnitude does not necessarily prove a zero effect.

## Reproducibility terms

**Result recalculation**

Recomputing published statistics from released fixed or anonymous projections.

**Classification audit**

Checking how birth data, chart structures, biographies, and action evidence produced the released classifications.

**End-to-end regeneration**

Rebuilding the complete chain from raw sources to charts, classifications, outcomes, and final statistics. The current public package does not provide this complete capability.

**Claim boundary**

A human- or machine-readable statement separating supported claims from unsupported interpretations for a specific release.
