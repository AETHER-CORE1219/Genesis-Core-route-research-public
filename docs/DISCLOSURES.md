# Research operator, commercial relationship, and contact

## Research operator

This public package is produced and maintained by **AETHERCORE Research**.

AETHERCORE develops systems that turn structured but contested bodies of knowledge into inspectable software and research workflows. Its current focus is GenesisCore and the empirical examination of astrology-related claims.

## Research position

AETHERCORE does not begin from a requirement that astrology must be true. The project reconstructs claims as calculable structures, compares them with real-world or participant data, and retains both positive and non-positive results.

The repository distinguishes:

- a chart calculation from evidence that a chart explains a person;
- an association from causality;
- result recalculation from end-to-end source reproduction;
- a development finding from independent person-out replication;
- a research hypothesis from a product claim.

## Commercial relationship

AETHERCORE also develops and operates **GenesisCore**, a paid Western/Jyotish personal analysis service. The public research, the service, and a separate voluntary participant study are connected parts of the same business and research program.

The purposes of public communication include:

- inviting methodological scrutiny and independent analysis;
- recruiting volunteers for the separate participant study;
- building public recognition of AETHERCORE and GenesisCore;
- improving the research and product through documented criticism.

Publication of the repository is not a request to endorse the paid service. Product usefulness, research association, prediction accuracy, and scientific validity must not be treated as interchangeable claims.

## Separate participant study

GenesisCore offers a free analysis path and an optional personality questionnaire. Participation in the research contribution is voluntary and is governed by separate research participation terms.

The participant study is not an independent replication of the 420-person public-figure analysis. Its population, outcomes, consent process, and research question differ.

## Funding and institutional status

The repository is published by AETHERCORE as an independently operated project. No university affiliation, journal peer review, institutional endorsement, or public-grant funding claim is made here.

This statement does not substitute for a complete future funding declaration. If external funding, institutional collaboration, or paid research support is added, this file should be updated in the same release that uses that relationship.
