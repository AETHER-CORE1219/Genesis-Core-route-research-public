# Post-v7 research status: historical results and later corrections

Status date: 2026-10-23. This is a documentation correction, not a new experiment or a reanalysis. The frozen v1-v7 data, code and reported results have not been replaced.

## Historical v7

The question is whether structures calculated from birth charts are associated with documented real-world actions. A chart-blind 731-person activity inventory and a fixed 420-person development analysis served different roles; neither count is the total number of independent validation cases, and they must not be added together.

In the same 420 people used to develop the candidates, historical v7 reported positive adjusted associations for five activity functions (risk differences +27.95 to +39.08 percentage points). These are differences in documented activity rates, not individual prediction accuracy. The reported significance values require independent statistical assessment. The fixed 22-test maxT procedure does not account for all freedom in the earlier rule-selection process. Re-running released calculations can reproduce the reported frozen statistics; it does not independently validate the statistical design or reconstruct every source-to-classification step.

## Later input and representation audit

The later audit changed calculation inputs for 74 of the same 420 people. On the portable representation, fixed-515 rule responses changed for 55 people. The larger disagreement between public AIZ and portable AJY responses (416 of 420 people) predates that correction-only comparison and must not be attributed solely to the input changes.

In the saved corrected portable fixed-515 evaluation, two of the five aggregate functions met the declared criterion: authored/symbolic production and organized collective realization. Advocacy and technology did not meet it; embodied competition could not be estimated because no one met that portable exposure definition. This does not establish that the broader embodied function is absent. These are saved audit results, not independently recalculated here. Their five-test family and representation differ from historical v7's 22-test analysis, so the two sets of p values are not directly comparable.

A separate symmetric rediscovery across Western and Jyotish produced zero confirmatory candidates across 11 functions under that version's feature universe and selection rules. This is a candidate-selection result, not evidence that every possible association is zero. It is also different from the fixed-515 evaluation above.

## Tests on other people

The three limited Jyotish formulations tested in v3-v5 did not meet their positive-confirmation criteria: reported risk differences were -2.56, +7.10 and -5.27 percentage points on the respective 575-, 1,457- and 313-person surfaces. They tested different units, were not direct repetitions of the headline both-system condition, and were not all wholly untouched cohorts. Positive person-out confirmation remains zero. This is an observed lack of positive confirmation in completed tests, not merely a test still waiting to be run.

## Product boundary

The five-path product matches fixed chart conditions and displays interpretations and reference cases. Neither a condition match nor a named reference person validates the individual rule, a person's ability, a success probability or future-event accuracy. The later two-of-five aggregate result does not validate a product score or automatically update a deployed classifier. Paid timing interpretations also have no established predictive validation from these studies.
