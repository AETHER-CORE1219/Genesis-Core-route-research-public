# Failures, limits, and prohibited interpretations

## Changes made after earlier research stages

- Occupation is no longer treated as one correct label. A person's documented activity is separated into several non-exclusive social functions.
- Embodied competition uses `any_tier_both_system` because the primary-plus-primary intersection was structurally zero. This surface was included in the fixed 22-surface family before the v7 result was inspected.
- External factors are added from M0 through M3 one at a time so that the change associated with each factor remains visible.
- The five headline candidates are not tested as five isolated hypotheses. Simultaneous correction uses the fixed 22-surface person-package maxT family.
- Adversarial decoys are not described as a random null. They are outcome-selected and used only as a necessary specificity challenge.

## Remaining limitations

1. The same development420 material contributed to candidate formation. Version 7 is not independent person-out replication.
2. The maxT family begins at the fixed 22 surfaces. It does not correct every degree of freedom involved in forming the earlier 741 rules, 515 rules, or 136 meaning families.
3. Sex or gender, region, birth-time quality, observation period, and related-person clusters are not directly adjusted because those variables are absent from the anonymous v7 matrix.
4. Outcomes measure actions found in fixed source material. A function not observed in that material cannot be treated as absent from the person's whole life.
5. Western/Jyotish ablation does not formally test a synergistic interaction between the two systems.
6. The linear probability model estimates an association in outcome rates. It does not identify an astrological causal mechanism.
7. Real-world activity classifications have not received an independent human replication.
8. The public projection supports statistical recalculation but not complete raw-source-to-classification regeneration.

## Relationship to v3, v4, and v5

The limited candidates tested in v3, v4, and v5 did not reach route-specific positive confirmation on their person-out surfaces.

Version 7 did not change their people, thresholds, or candidates. It asks a different question: how far the five fixed aggregate development associations survive measured external factors and multiplicity across a declared family.

The correct combined reading is therefore:

- the five aggregate associations remain positive within development420 after the v7 checks;
- three particular formulations did not receive positive person-out confirmation in v3–v5;
- no individual route has yet received positive person-out confirmation;
- the non-positive tests do not establish a universal null for every family or for astrology as a whole.

## Prohibited interpretations

Do not describe v7 as showing that:

- independent replication has been achieved;
- astrology has been proved scientifically or causally;
- every unmeasured confound has been excluded;
- all 515 rules or 136 meaning families have been validated;
- Western and Jyotish have a confirmed interaction effect;
- a birth chart predicts a person's occupation, behavior, or future;
- field and occupation were the labels used to construct the five outcomes;
- a missing function in the source material is absent from the person's life;
- the adversarial decoys are a random null distribution;
- the complete earlier candidate search is corrected by the fixed-22 maxT test.

## Accurate short statement

> In the fixed 420-person development analysis, all five aggregate chart-structure-to-social-function associations remained positive after adjustment for birth era, measurement regime, and collection source, and after simultaneous correction across the fixed 22-surface family. This is a development-sample robustness result, not independent person-out replication or causal proof.
