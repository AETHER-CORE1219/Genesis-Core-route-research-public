# Independent social-expression route study v4

## Result

This release evaluates one frozen aggregate candidate: the **Jyotish Mars–Mercury organized-realization two-branch family**. It is a social-action hypothesis, not an occupation classifier.

The conditional person-out frame contained 1,495 people. The primary analysis used the 1,457 people represented by independent person pages: 59 family-exposed and 1,398 unexposed. Among decided observations, direct documented organized-action anchors were found for 35/58 exposed and 739/1,378 unexposed people.

The language × birth-era × fixed-text-length stratified estimate was `0.0710069495`. Its HC3 one-sided 95% lower and upper bounds were `-0.0292741910` and `0.1712880901`. The one-sided within-stratum permutation p-value was `0.1357` over 9,999 draws; the empirical p-value against 98 frozen complexity/prevalence-matched decoys was `0.131313`.

The predeclared development-sized reference effect was `0.1993299573`, with minimum conditional power `0.906176759`. The upper bound fell below that reference effect while the positive-confirmation rule failed. The terminal result is therefore **`ADEQUATELY_POWERED_PRIMARY_NULL`**:

> The predeclared development-sized effect did not reproduce. A smaller positive difference remains unconfirmed, not excluded.

This is not evidence of zero association, a rejection of astrology as a whole, individual validation or rejection of either branch or the 515 development rules, Western/Jyotish convergence, causality, or occupation prediction.

## Confirmation boundary

This is a conditional person-out confirmation, not a fully untouched external cohort. Source identity, birth metadata, and older prediction surfaces had prior exposure; the social-action semantics/outcomes used here remained blind to family membership until the single late join.

The endpoint is a person's action documented in fixed source material, not the person's whole life. Rating used one AI system and has not yet received independent human replication.
