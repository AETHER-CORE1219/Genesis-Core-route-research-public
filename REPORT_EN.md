# GenesisCore social-expression route research

## Abstract

This repository investigates whether structured Western and Jyotish chart classifications are associated with actions documented in people's lives. The wider route-research program tracks 1,030 notable public figures, including 795 exact-like birth records and 761 public subject-level legacy traces. A later research line replaces single occupational labels with five non-exclusive social functions and evaluates versioned development and person-out surfaces.

The strongest current positive result is a fixed 420-person development-sample robustness analysis. All five aggregate social-function associations remained positive after sequential adjustment for birth era, reality-measurement regime, and collection source. Adjusted risk differences ranged from +27.95 to +39.08 percentage points. All five primary surfaces also passed simultaneous correction in a 9,999-draw person-package maxT analysis across a fixed 22-surface family.

The result is not independent person-out replication. Three narrower candidate formulations tested in versions 3–5 did not receive route-specific positive confirmation on separate-person surfaces. The public package preserves those non-positive results, the positive development robustness result, anonymous analysis projections, machine-readable claim boundaries, and deterministic reproduction scripts.

## 1. Research position

The project does not begin with a requirement to prove astrology true. It asks which astrology-related claims can be converted into fixed, inspectable definitions and what happens when those definitions are compared with documented human activity.

Several distinctions govern the research:

- a chart can be calculated without proving that it explains a person;
- a statistical association is not a causal mechanism;
- a development result is not an independent replication;
- result recalculation is not complete raw-source regeneration;
- a person's occupation is not the same as the set of actions performed across a life;
- a failed narrow hypothesis is not automatically a null result for every related hypothesis or for astrology as a whole.

The purpose of the public repository is to let supporters, skeptics, researchers, and developers inspect the same frozen record.

## 2. Two connected research lines

### 2.1 Legacy route research

The legacy research uses birth-data records and public biographical material from notable figures in the arts, science and technology, business, sport, politics, humanities, religion, activism, exploration, and other fields.

The current public snapshot reports:

- master subjects: 1,030;
- exact-like subjects: 795;
- subject-level discovery traces: 761;
- fixed public route claims: 16;
- unified non-claim rows: 14;
- science non-fixed rows: 16;
- one surviving narrow packet candidate;
- eight provisional watch subbranches.

The 16 fixed items are route families or branches retained in the public claim surface. `Fixed` means frozen for that stated surface; it does not mean universally true, independently replicated, or suitable as a deterministic profession classifier.

The public 761-person trace connects names, fields, birth-data provenance, selected chart facts, and route-pressure observations. A top route in this trace is not a final identity label for the person.

### 2.2 Five non-exclusive social functions

The later research changes the outcome design. A person is not forced into one occupation or one social role. The analysis instead permits several documented functions for the same person:

1. public advocacy and public-cause action;
2. authored or symbolic production;
3. organized collective realization;
4. technology or product implementation;
5. embodied competitive performance.

This structure reflects the fact that a public figure may write, organize, advocate, implement, and compete in different parts of the same life.

## 3. Data surfaces and population boundaries

The wider 1,030-person legacy corpus and the five-function releases are related parts of one research program, but the public release does not provide a simple row-level mapping among every surface.

`development420` is the fixed development analysis used to construct and study the five aggregate functions. Versions 3–5 then test particular candidate formulations on separate-person surfaces:

- v3: 575 people for one broad authored-production aggregate;
- v4: a fixed 1,495-person frame with 1,457 people in the primary analysis for one organized-realization family;
- v5: 313 people in a legacy person-separated surface for one strict embodied-competition branch.

These counts cannot be summed to obtain a total number of unique research participants. They differ in source, eligibility, hypothesis, and release boundary.

## 4. Real-world outcomes

The intended outcome unit is a person's action documented in fixed source material. It separates actor, action, object or output, and carrier or social setting.

Where available, evidence states distinguish supported, partial, contested, source-thin, and unknown records. A missing action in the fixed source is not interpreted as proof that the action never occurred in the person's life.

Field and occupation are not used to determine whether a person belongs to one of the five functions. They may be used as descriptive, covariate, moderator, or transport variables only.

This decision addresses a central problem in occupation-based astrology studies: one occupational label can hide several materially different actions, while the same type of action can appear across unrelated professions.

## 5. Astrology-side structure

Western and Jyotish frameworks are structured separately. They are not treated as two interchangeable votes.

The research hierarchy distinguishes:

1. **Function group** — what the person is documented to have done.
2. **Meaning or mechanism family** — a shared functional role and actor capability represented by a bounded astrological meaning core.
3. **Finite route branch** — a particular relation, direction, carrier, and modifier structure within the family.
4. **Person interpretation** — a whole-chart explanation of how several branches and several documented activities coexist in one person.

Group-level definitions must not be rewritten after observing an individual result. A person interpretation cannot be used to rescue a failed group test.

Concrete public Western and Jyotish candidate forms—including MC-ruler, A10-lord, Amatya, relation, house, D10, and Dusthana structures—are summarized in [Astrological Structure Examples](docs/ASTROLOGICAL_STRUCTURE_EXAMPLES.md).

In v7, each function has predefined Western, Jyotish, and both-system surfaces. The first four headline functions use `pp_both_system`. Embodied competition uses `any_tier_both_system` because the stricter primary-plus-primary intersection was structurally zero. That choice was included in the fixed 22-surface family before the v7 result was inspected.

## 6. Versioned research history

### 6.1 Version 1: measurement canary

A 12-person canary tested whether the proposed real-world measurement was ready for an external route test. It ended in `MEASUREMENT_FAILURE`. No external route validation was performed.

This failure is important because it prevented an unstable outcome measure from being interpreted as evidence for or against the route hypothesis.

### 6.2 Version 2: development candidates

Version 2 constructed the fixed development420 surface, five non-exclusive functions, 515 rules, and the measurement actor gate. It reported development candidates but an external route validation count of zero.

### 6.3 Version 3: broad authored aggregate

Version 3 tested a corrected Jyotish authored-production aggregate containing 25 units in a separate 575-person surface. The era-standardized risk difference was approximately -2.56 percentage points, the permutation p-value was approximately 0.6923, and the Holm-adjusted p-value was 1.0.

The broad aggregate did not reproduce. The result is a null for that heterogeneous aggregate, not for every authored-production family. The other four functions did not pass the predeclared power gate for this test.

### 6.4 Version 4: organized-realization family

Version 4 tested one frozen Jyotish Mars–Mercury organized-realization two-branch family. The primary analysis used 1,457 independent person pages: 59 exposed and 1,398 unexposed.

The adjusted estimate was approximately +7.10 percentage points. The one-sided permutation p-value was 0.1357 and the matched-decoy empirical p-value was approximately 0.1313. The analysis had more than 0.90 minimum conditional power for the predeclared development-sized reference effect of approximately +19.93 percentage points, and the upper confidence bound fell below that reference.

The terminal result was `ADEQUATELY_POWERED_PRIMARY_NULL`: the development-sized effect did not reproduce. A smaller positive difference remained unconfirmed rather than established or excluded.

### 6.5 Version 5: strict embodied branch

Version 5 applied one strict Jyotish embodied-competition chain to a separate 313-person legacy surface. The primary adjusted risk difference was approximately -5.27 percentage points, with permutation p-value 1.0 and decoy p-value 0.63.

Removing the strict carrier returned an adjusted direction of approximately +3.38 percentage points, but its permutation p-value was 0.5276. The strict route was non-positive and the less restrictive direction remained unconfirmed.

### 6.6 Version 6: synthesis and granularity

Version 6 did not perform a new inferential test. It integrated the development findings, 136 corrected meaning families, person-level traces, and the v3–v5 stress tests.

Its main methodological conclusion concerns granularity. A heterogeneous `ANY` aggregate can be too broad, while one all-condition branch can be too narrow. The proposed future test unit is a finite, meaning-coherent mechanism family with predeclared alternative relation and carrier branches.

### 6.7 Version 7: measured-factor and multiplicity robustness

Version 7 returned to the fixed development420 surface. It did not alter the v3–v5 person-out results. Its question was whether measured external factors or simultaneous testing across a fixed family could explain the five aggregate development associations.

## 7. Version 7 methods

For person `i`, `Y_i` represents support for a documented function and `E_i` represents fixed chart-structure exposure. A linear probability model reports the coefficient of `E_i` as a risk difference with HC3 standard errors.

The factors are added sequentially:

```text
M0: Y ~ 1 + E
M1: M0 + birth era
M2: M1 + measurement regime
M3: M2 + collection source
```

The release also examines:

- raw and permuted differences within birth-era levels;
- continuous birth decade with a quadratic term;
- direction in the two main measurement regimes;
- source-group estimates and leave-one-source-out estimates;
- within-source permutations;
- observation-opportunity adjustment in the 311- and 109-person development subcohorts;
- descriptive Western-only, Jyotish-only, and full both-system estimates;
- sensitivity quantities for unmeasured confounding.

The fixed family contains 22 surfaces, 18 of which are estimable. A 9,999-draw person-package maxT procedure shares each random sign across all functions for the same person and retains the largest t statistic across the estimable surfaces. This preserves within-person dependence while addressing multiplicity in the fixed family.

The release also compares candidates with 7,634 weak or reverse-direction predicate rows representing 708 unique exposure sets. Because these decoys were selected using outcome information, they are not a random null. They are used only as a necessary specificity challenge.

## 8. Version 7 results

| Function | M3 adjusted risk difference | Simultaneous lower bound | Adjusted maxT p-value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Public advocacy | +0.33596 | +0.18478 | 0.0001 |
| Authored production | +0.39080 | +0.27786 | 0.0001 |
| Organized collective realization | +0.27954 | +0.16011 | 0.0001 |
| Technology or product implementation | +0.32494 | +0.09643 | 0.0008 |
| Embodied competition | +0.32140 | +0.06930 | 0.0047 |

All five primary results exceeded both declared adversarial-decoy 95th-percentile challenges. The direction also remained positive in the declared factor-specific checks.

Within development420 and the fixed 22-surface null, chance alone is an implausible explanation for the five-function pattern, and the three measured factors do not explain the associations away.

## 9. Interpretation

The v7 result strengthens the description of the five-function development pattern. It shows that the pattern is not removed merely by adding the measured birth-era, measurement-regime, and collection-source variables or by correcting across the declared fixed family.

It does not settle the larger scientific question. The same development material contributed to candidate formation, and the fixed-22 correction does not encompass every choice involved in earlier candidate construction. Important variables are absent from the anonymous matrix. The reality-side classifications do not yet have independent human replication. The public release cannot regenerate every classification from raw sources.

The proper research response is therefore neither to dismiss the positive result nor to promote it as final proof. It is to preserve the result, preserve the failed tests, improve provenance and classification auditability, and conduct predeclared replication on new people.

## 10. Public reproducibility

The repository provides:

- versioned result files;
- anonymous analysis ledgers and matrices;
- machine-readable claim boundaries;
- deterministic reproduction scripts;
- checksum manifests;
- privacy validators for v4, v5, and v7;
- named legacy subject traces and source manifests.

The cross-platform public verifier can be run from the repository root:

```bash
python -m pip install -r requirements-public.txt
python scripts/verify_public_release.py
```

The public package supports result recalculation from released projections. It does not provide complete end-to-end regeneration from raw birth and biographical sources. See [Reproducibility Boundary](docs/REPRODUCIBILITY_BOUNDARY.md).

## 11. Data and documentation limitations

- The v7 420-person matrix is anonymous and has no public identity map.
- The 761 named legacy traces are not a v7 identity lookup table.
- Some legacy trace rows lack a public biography or birth-source URL.
- Historical absolute build paths remain in the legacy trace and are not public evidence links.
- The source opportunity and classification process may differ across people and eras.
- Related-person clusters are not fully identified for cluster-adjusted inference.
- The development candidate universe predates the fixed 22-surface family.
- No current release establishes predictive accuracy for individual users.

## 12. Next research steps

The strongest next research design would:

1. freeze a finite, meaning-coherent family before examining new outcomes;
2. define a new source and person frame before chart exposure is joined;
3. obtain independent human ratings of documented actions;
4. preserve partial, contested, source-thin, and unknown states;
5. predeclare multiplicity, missingness, and related-person handling;
6. release an auditable source manifest and deterministic chart-generation path where licensing and privacy permit;
7. report positive, non-positive, and measurement-failure outcomes under separate versioned claim boundaries.
