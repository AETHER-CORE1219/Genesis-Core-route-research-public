# Independent social-expression route study v3

This release reports the one held-out evaluation of a corrected Jyotish mechanism family for `AUTHORED_SYMBOLIC_PRODUCTION`.

Among 575 previously unused people, 218 matched at least one of 25 frozen corrected units and 357 did not. The analyzable comparison contained 127 exposed and 212 unexposed people. The era-standardized risk difference was `-0.02562` (bootstrap 95% interval `[-0.12690, 0.07693]`); the five-candidate Holm-adjusted p-value was `1.0`.

The aggregate family did not reproduce out of person. The other four functions were not tested because their predeclared power was insufficient. This is not a test of individual legacy rules, Western/Jyotish convergence, causality, or occupation prediction.
